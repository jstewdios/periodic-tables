Odd Sympathy

In 1665 Huygens, sick in bed, saw two clocks on one beam swinging in step. He knocked them apart. Within half an hour they had found each other again.

Each ring here is a clock. Its size sets its tempo and its note. Every tick sends out a ripple that tugs on its neighbours.
